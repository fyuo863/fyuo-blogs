# Earth texture attribution

`blue-marble-1024.png` is NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio's Blue Marble mosaic (SVS ID 2915), derived from Terra/Aqua MODIS observations.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/2915/

Credit requested by source: NASA/GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio; Blue Marble Next Generation data courtesy of Reto Stockli (NASA/GSFC) and NASA's Earth Observatory.

`black-marble-2012.jpg` is NASA Earth Observatory's Black Marble global nighttime-light map (2012), derived from Suomi NPP VIIRS day-night-band observations.

Source: https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/images/79803/night-lights-2012-the-black-marble

Credit: NASA Earth Observatory images by Joshua Stevens, using Suomi NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Román, NASA GSFC.
